08 stagesExample configuration. Project-specific scope.Scroll to assemble ↓
01

Scope

Choose one critical service.

Agree a service boundary, the responsible team and the operational questions the pilot should help answer.

02

Dependencies

See what the service relies on.

Map the selected assets, power, connectivity and software dependencies. Record gaps and assumptions for the team to check.

03

Private environment

Prepare the monitoring home.

Use agreed customer-controlled compute. Define access, retention and integration boundaries before connecting operational information.

04

Integration

Connect the signals you can use.

Bring permitted telemetry, logs and records together through available interfaces. Validate timestamps, coverage and data quality.

05

Baseline

Learn what normal looks like.

Establish a reviewed baseline from available history and operating context. Configure analysis around the selected service and known limitations.

06

Decision support

Connect a signal to its impact.

Flag possible anomalies with supporting evidence and dependency context. Engineers decide which findings need investigation or action.

07

Resilience review

Test the response safely.

Evaluate isolation and recovery options using agreed scenarios or a controlled test environment. Do not assume authority to alter live systems.

08

Evaluation

Review the evidence. Decide next.

Assess useful findings, missed context, false alerts and team workload against agreed criteria. Decide whether to refine, extend or stop the pilot.
