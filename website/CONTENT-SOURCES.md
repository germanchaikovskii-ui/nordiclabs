## IAEA configuration-management foundation

- [SSR-2/1 (Rev. 1), Requirement 3, paragraph 3.6](https://nucleus.iaea.org/sites/nss-oui/Published%20Collections/m_daad92bb-3bb0-4445-a6ec-a287e55e9664/m_daad92bb-3bb0-4445-a6ec-a287e55e9664__65_0.Html): retained design knowledge, requirements/configuration control and documented design changes.
- [SSR-2/2 (Rev. 1), Requirement 10, paragraph 4.38, printed page 15](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/Pub1716web-18398071.pdf#page=35): consistency among requirements, plant documentation and physical configuration; controlled changes.
- [Safety Reports Series No. 65, Section 2.1](https://nucleus.iaea.org/sites/connect/MSNpublic/Pages/nct/downloads/Pub1461_web.pdf#page=16): the configuration-management equilibrium model and supporting practical guidance. This is a Safety Report, not a separate mandatory requirement.

Primary sources were checked directly. The website paraphrases them and links to the original publications. Its five-step evidence chain is our implementation concept, not a prescribed IAEA architecture. The site does not claim IAEA endorsement, software certification or automatic regulatory approval. Applicable references depend on the national framework, licensing basis and contractual commitments.

## Lifecycle and contractor scope

The page follows the sequence explicitly requested by the owner: EPC requirements, licensing/delivery plans, Basic Design, PSAR and safety analyses before the construction-licence milestone; Detailed Design, procurement, deliveries, work execution, acceptance and FSAR evidence during construction; operation and modifications after the operating-licence milestone. The actual authorization conditions remain project-specific.

Before construction licensing, commencement criteria and acceptance/accounting interfaces are defined. During construction, these workflows track execution. A commencement gate is a contract/work-package release decision, separate from the two regulatory licensing milestones. AI agents support review; designated staff authorize work release, acceptance and financial actions.
