Health Intelligence API

Turn Health Data Into Actionable Intelligence.

A proposed partner API for health and fitness services. Share authorized user observations and receive individualized insights into how meals, exercise and rest relate to that person’s hunger, energy and recovery.

01 · Send observations02 · Analyze responses03 · Receive insights

01 / Partner inputs

Connect the signals your service already collects.

A nutrition service could send meal times and composition. A training platform could send session duration and intensity. A wearable service could contribute sleep or activity observations.

For each integration, we agree which fields the user authorizes for the purpose. Source and time travel with each observation so the analysis can reconstruct the sequence.

02 / ARC Engine analysis

Understand the response in context.

ARC Engine relates each event to what followed for the same person. For example, it can compare hunger check-ins after longer workouts with check-ins after lighter sessions, taking meal timing into account.

Repeated observations give a pattern more weight. Where the record is sparse, the result can identify the missing context and suggest a follow-up question.

03 / Results for your platform

Bring the insight back to the user.

A partner could receive an estimated hunger window, a recovery-aware training suggestion or a summary of changes in reported energy. Each result would include the observations that informed it and any gaps that affect interpretation.

Your service chooses how to present the result. With permission, the user’s next check-in feeds back into the analysis.
