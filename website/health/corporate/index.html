01 / For employees

A personal plan for the working day.

Food around a shift. ESIVA helps employees plan meals around their schedule and see how the day’s food fits their own goals.

Activity that fits. A short session between shifts or a lighter workout after poor sleep can keep a routine manageable.

Control over personal information. Employees choose whether to participate. Their meal logs, workouts and individual health records stay private.

02 / For employers

One benefit for people with different routines.

Support across teams. HR can sponsor the same benefit for office staff and shift workers while each employee follows a plan suited to their day.

Evidence for renewal. Voluntary feedback can help HR assess uptake, usefulness and barriers to participation before expanding. Over time, the organization can evaluate potential effects on productivity, absence and costs.

Any future employer reporting would require appropriate consent, authorization and aggregation that protects participants’ identities.

03 / Program launch

Start with a team and a clear review date.

Define the scope. Agree on the teams to invite, the program’s goals, sponsorship, privacy arrangements and commercial terms.

Invite and onboard. Explain how employees can opt in, get access, set personal goals and reach support.

Review together. At the agreed date, review participation and voluntary feedback, then decide whether to adjust, extend or expand the program.
