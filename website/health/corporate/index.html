Healthier Teams. Stronger Organizations.

Explore ESIVA-based personal guidance as part of an employee wellbeing program, with a rollout designed around your people and their working day.

Wellbeing that fits the working day

Meals and movement
An employee can plan meals around a shift, choose an activity goal and fit a workout into the time available.
Recovery and daily guidance
Sleep, recent activity and personal goals give ESIVA context for daily suggestions. Small adjustments help employees build routines they can sustain.
What an employer can evaluate
Start with participation and engagement with healthy routines. Over time, evaluate whether the program supports wellbeing and productivity, reduces health-related absence or affects healthcare and insurance costs.

A program built around your organization

Access and onboarding
HR can sponsor employee access. Together we would agree on the participating teams, onboarding, support and commercial terms.
Employee privacy
Personal records, nutrition logs and workout histories stay private. Any future reporting would require appropriate consent and authorization, privacy-preserving aggregation and safeguards against identifying individuals, consistent with applicable data-protection requirements.
