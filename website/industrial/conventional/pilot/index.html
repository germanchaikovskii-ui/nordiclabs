A working system.
Built around your process.

Start with one process. Connect the information it needs. See how an AI agent could support the people who make the decisions.

Build the pilot, step by step ↓
08 stagesYour infrastructure. Your records. Your team.Scroll to assemble ↓
01

Infrastructure

Start with a place for your data.

Deploy a dedicated server, use your existing infrastructure or choose a controlled cloud environment. Agree access and security before connecting data.

WHAT YOU HAVE

An agreed environment for the pilot.

02

Pilot scope

One process. A clear starting point.

Choose a specific business process with your team. Define the question the pilot should answer and the evidence you will use to evaluate it.

WHAT YOU HAVE

A bounded workflow and agreed success criteria.

03

Your AI agent

Give the agent your working context.

Configure the agent around your procedures, terminology and approved records. Your specialists review example responses and help refine its behaviour.

WHAT YOU HAVE

An agent configured for the selected process.

04

Connected processes

Bring the dependencies into view.

Identify the adjacent workflows that affect the pilot. Agree which teams, records and hand-offs belong in scope.

WHAT YOU HAVE

A map of the processes that need to connect.

05

Information layer

Make the information usable.

Bring approved records into the agreed environment. Structure documents, preserve source references and check access permissions and data quality.

WHAT YOU HAVE

A traceable information base for analysis.

06

Project integration

Connect a project to its business context.

Link the agreed project records with procurement, warehouse, deliveries and finance. Available interfaces and permissions determine the integration scope.

WHAT YOU HAVE

A connected view of the selected project.

07

Working dashboards

The right view for each decision.

Give specialists a review queue, project managers a delivery view and leaders a summary of priorities. Each view leads back to the supporting records.

WHAT YOU HAVE

Role-specific dashboards for your teams.

08

Evaluation

See what changes in daily work.

Evaluate the pilot with specialists, managers and leaders against the agreed baseline. Review usefulness, errors and time spent, then decide what to improve or extend.

WHAT YOU HAVE

Evidence for the next decision—not an automatic rollout.
