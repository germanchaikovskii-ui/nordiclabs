Your robotics pilot.
One piece at a time.

Build one practical inspection pilot: a server, an AI agent, one drone—and findings your team can review.

Build the pilot, step by step ↓
08 stagesYour infrastructure. Your records. Your team.Scroll to assemble ↓
01

Infrastructure

A home for your robotics AI.

We set up a server—or use your chosen infrastructure—to hold the pilot’s information and run the agreed AI workflows.

02

AI setup

Prepare the inspection agent.

We configure the AI around your inspection task, site documents and review rules. Your specialists check its outputs.

03

Pilot mission

Choose one area. One task.

Select a bounded inspection: for example, recording construction progress in one area. Agree what to capture and how to judge the result.

04

Pilot equipment

Start with one drone.

Agree one drone type and its supply or rental terms. Plan the camera, operator, site access and flight permissions before deployment.

05

Site observations

Capture the real site.

An authorised operator carries out the agreed mission. Images and observations are transferred to the pilot environment.

06

AI-assisted review

Turn observations into findings.

Compare the captured evidence with agreed plans or previous surveys. AI flags possible differences for a specialist to verify.

07

Team workspace

Give the team a shared view.

Specialists review evidence. Managers track findings and follow-up. Leaders see the agreed pilot summary.

08

Pilot evaluation

Review the result. Decide next.

Evaluate coverage, evidence quality and usefulness with your team. Agree whether to refine the mission, expand it or stop.
