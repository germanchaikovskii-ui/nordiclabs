Privacy Policy

How we handle website visits, business enquiries and your personal information.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Nordic Labs Tech Oy, Finland, Business ID (Y-tunnus) 3668654-2, is the controller for personal data we process in connection with this corporate website and business enquiries. Contact us at info@nordiclabs.tech for privacy questions or requests.

This policy covers nordiclabs.tech and related correspondence. It does not govern use of the ESIVA application, which has separate privacy information on the ESIVA website. Project-specific data processing is addressed in the relevant project documentation and agreements.

2. Information and how we receive it

Business enquiries. When you email us, we receive your email address, the name and organisation you provide, your area of interest, message content, attachments and associated correspondence details. We use the information you choose to share to understand and respond to your enquiry.

Contact form. The form prepares an email draft locally in your browser. It does not submit your entries to our server or store them in website cookies or browser storage. Opening the draft passes its contents to your email application; copying it places the contents on your clipboard. We receive the enquiry when you send the email. Your browser and email provider may handle drafts or autofill under their own settings and policies.

Website visits. The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and processes technical information associated with serving the website. Details of its processing, retention and international transfers appear in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Providing enquiry information is voluntary. Without sufficient contact details and context, we may be unable to respond or assess a proposed project. Please do not include health data, identity documents or confidential project material in an initial enquiry.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Responding to enquiries and discussing business opportunities: our legitimate interests in communicating with prospective customers and business contacts, balanced against your rights (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Taking steps at your request before a contract, or performing a contract with you personally: GDPR Article 6(1)(b). Where you represent an organisation, we generally process your business contact details on the legitimate-interest basis above.
  • Meeting legal obligations: GDPR Article 6(1)(c), where record-keeping or disclosure is required by law.
  • Protecting our systems and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims: our legitimate interests under GDPR Article 6(1)(f).

Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list. This website does not make decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects solely through automated processing.

4. Cookies and tracking

Our website code does not set cookies, use advertising trackers or run third-party audience analytics. It does not store contact-form entries in local storage or session storage. Hosting security logs are separate from cookies. Following an external link takes you to a site with its own privacy and cookie practices.

5. Access, providers and international transfers

Enquiry information is accessible to people authorised to handle the correspondence and to service providers supporting email and IT operations. We may disclose relevant information to professional advisers or authorities where necessary to meet legal obligations or protect legal rights. We do not sell personal data.

Hosting and communication services can involve processing outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. GitHub describes its international processing and safeguards in its privacy statement. Where we arrange a transfer requiring safeguards under the GDPR, we use an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, such as European Commission standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about recipients and the safeguards relevant to your data, or to request a copy of applicable safeguards.

6. Retention and protection

We retain enquiry correspondence while it is needed to address the request and any related project discussions. Once that purpose ends, we delete information unless it remains necessary for a contractual relationship, a legal record-keeping obligation or a potential or ongoing legal claim. Retention is determined by the status of the enquiry, the records required for the relationship and applicable legal retention or limitation periods. GitHub determines retention for its own hosting security records under its privacy statement.

Access and handling are limited to what is appropriate for these purposes. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure. Before sharing sensitive or restricted project information, contact us to arrange suitable protection and transfer methods.

7. Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction of processing and, where applicable, data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your situation. If processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send requests to info@nordiclabs.tech. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity. We normally respond within one month; if a lawful extension is needed, we will explain it within that period.

You may lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority, including Finland’s Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.

8. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our website or relevant data practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. Where required, we will provide additional information about material changes.
